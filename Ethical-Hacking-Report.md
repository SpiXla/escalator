# Ethical Hacking Report

## Authorization

Ethical hacking must only be performed with proper authorization. Before testing begins, the tester must have explicit permission from the owner of the target system and must understand the exact scope of the engagement. Without authorization, the same actions would be unethical and could be illegal.

## Legal and Ethical Boundaries

Vulnerability testing must stay within legal and ethical limits. The goal is to identify and validate security weaknesses without causing unnecessary disruption, data loss, or harm to systems and users. Testing should be limited to what is necessary to demonstrate the issue, and unrelated data or services should not be accessed without a clear reason and permission.

## Responsible Disclosure

Vulnerabilities should be reported responsibly and privately to the appropriate stakeholders. A good report should explain the issue clearly, describe the security impact, and provide enough evidence to support remediation while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of full exploitation details. The purpose of ethical hacking is to improve security, not to create more risk.
